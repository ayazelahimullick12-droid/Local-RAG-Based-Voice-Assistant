# Benchmark samples

Drop 16kHz mono `.wav` recordings of Bengali speech here for `scripts/benchmark.py`.

Include a mix:
- Clean, clearly-spoken single sentences (best case)
- Natural conversational speech — filler words, self-corrections, code-switched English terms
- A couple of Bangladeshi-dialect recordings if you're A/B testing the NeMo Conformer fallback
- At least one recording with numbers, dates, or currency in it, to sanity-check normalization

None are included in this repo — record your own or source them separately;
nothing here should be assumed to exist until you add it.
